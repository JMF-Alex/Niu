Privacy and terms

Last updated 23 November 2026. Niu is a student project; these texts describe how the demo behaves.

Privacy policy

When you reserve a trial we store your email address, the plan you chose and the date. When you use the contact form we store your name, email, topic and message. We use this only to reply to you. We do not sell or share it.

The site sets no cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

To have your data deleted, write to the project team through the contact page and we remove it within 30 days.

Terms of service

Niu is offered as a demonstration. No real hosting is provisioned and no payment is taken. Prices, uptime figures, customers and quotes on the site are illustrative.

Do not submit personal data that is not yours.

FAQ

Questions before you sign up

Which versions of Node can I run?

Node 20, 22 and 24. Set the version in the engines field of your package.json. Static sites from any framework that builds to HTML also work.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. You get 30 days of Pro. When it ends you choose a plan or your project moves to Hobby automatically.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on Pro and Team. Add a CNAME record pointing at your project and we issue the HTTPS certificate within a few minutes.

Where is my data stored?

Apps and databases run in our Madrid data center. Encrypted backups are copied to Frankfurt. Nothing leaves the EU.

What happens if I go over my plan limits?

We email you at 80% of any limit. Your site keeps running and we never suspend a project without 7 days of notice.

Can I cancel and take my data with me?

Yes. Billing is monthly, you can cancel from the dashboard at any time, and you can export your database as a standard mongodump archive.

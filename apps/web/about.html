Our story

From a classroom project to a hosting company

Every final project at our school ended the same way: a working app on a laptop and no good place to put it. Free tiers slept, VPS setups ate weekends, and the big platforms billed in dollars from servers far away.

So we built the platform we needed. One command to deploy, a database that is already there, and servers close enough that a page in Lleida loads before you blink.

Today Niu hosts shops, booking systems and hundreds of student projects, and every teacher who asks still gets free accounts for their class.

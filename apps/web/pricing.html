Billing

Questions about paying

What happens when the 30 day trial ends?

If you have not added a payment method, your project moves to Hobby. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade again whenever you like.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and SEPA direct debit. Companies on Team can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice.

Do you issue invoices with VAT?

Yes. Add your company name and tax ID in the dashboard and every invoice includes them. EU businesses with a valid VAT number are billed under reverse charge.

Is there a discount for schools?

Teachers can request free Pro accounts for a whole class. Write to us and choose the education topic.
